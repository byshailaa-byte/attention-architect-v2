Meera fights hardest over screens.

Here’s why, and it isn’t laziness.

In one line

Good: Meera locks onto hard things and goes all the way in.

Why: Screens match how Meera's brain works — deep, intense, total.

Fix: Build a wind-down gap before the screen-off moment.

Why the screens doesn’t stick

Meera's focus works like a storm. It builds fast, goes deep, and needs time to slow down before it stops.

Cutting a screen off mid-storm feels like a sudden crash. That crash is what causes the battle, not the rule.

What we saw in your answers

You said what pulls Meera in is: “Going deep into one thing, and ignoring everything else”

You said what lights Meera up is: “Figuring out something really hard”

You said what pulls Meera away is: “Noise, movement, or activity nearby”

Put together, these three answers are what pointed us to Meera’s pattern.

Meera’s pattern

The Storm

Bursts of intense focus, then a strong need to step back and reset.

The one switch

Instead of: Screen off now, please.

Try: Five more minutes, then we wind down.

Meera gets a small exit ramp, and the battle shrinks.

Try it tonight

  1. Pick one show or game Meera is using tonight.
  2. Set a timer for five minutes before you want it off.
  3. When the timer goes, say: 'Wind-down time — screens off in five.'

Your goal

Screens off without a battle.
