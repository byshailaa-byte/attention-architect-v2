Meera fights hardest over screens.

Here’s why, and it isn’t laziness.

In one line

Good: Meera can lock in hard when something grabs her.

Why: Screens are fast and sure, so slow work feels like a loss.

Fix: Set a clear off-ramp before the screen goes on.

Why the screens doesn’t stick

When it is Meera’s idea, she will stay with it. The moment it is handed over already decided, the same energy turns into a no.

That is why screens win. They are fast and sure, so the swap to slow work feels like a loss.

What we saw in your answers

You said what pulls Meera in is: “Going deep into one thing, and ignoring everything else”

You said what lights Meera up is: “Figuring out something really hard”

You said what pulls Meera away is: “Noise, movement, or activity nearby”

Put together, these three answers are what pointed us to Meera’s pattern.

Meera’s pattern

The Storm

Bursts of intense focus, then a strong need to step back and reset.

The one switch

Instead of: “Here’s your homework — start with this one.”

Try: “Two ways to start — you pick which.”

Then step back and let Meera’s choice stand.

Try it tonight

  1. Before homework, give Meera two ways to start.
  2. Let her pick, and let the choice stand.
  3. Watch the first five minutes, not the whole session.

Your goal

Screens off without a battle.
