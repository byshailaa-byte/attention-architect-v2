Meera fights hardest over screens.

Here’s why, and it isn’t laziness.

In one line

Good: Nothing is wrong with Meera. She goes deep and knows her own mind.

Why: "Screens off now" is your decision, not hers — so she fights it.

Fix: Let her pick the stop time before screens go on. 5 minutes a day.

Why the screens doesn’t stick

You told us Meera goes deep into one thing and ignores everything else. That focus is real. But it only switches on when the plan is hers.

When you call time on the screen, she didn't choose that. So that same energy turns into a no.

What we saw in your answers

You said what pulls Meera in is: “Going deep into one thing, and ignoring everything else”

You said what lights Meera up is: “Figuring out something really hard”

You said what pulls Meera away is: “Noise, movement, or activity nearby”

Put together, these three answers are what pointed us to Meera’s pattern.

Meera’s pattern

The Storm

Big bursts of focus, then a strong need to step back.

The one switch

Instead of: "Meera, screens off. Now. I mean it."

Try: "Off at 7, or after this episode? You pick."

Then let her choice stand, even if it runs a little late.

Try it tonight

  1. Before the screen goes on, offer two stop times. Let her choose one.
  2. Write it where she can see it — her words, her time.
  3. When the time comes, just point to what she chose. Say nothing else.

Your goal

Screens off without a battle.
