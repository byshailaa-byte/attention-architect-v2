Kabir turns homework into a daily fight.

Here’s why, and it isn’t laziness.

In one line

Good: Kabir works hard and long when someone he trusts is nearby.

Why: Homework falls apart alone because Kabir's attention runs on connection.

Fix: Sit near him with your own task. Don't help — just be there.

Why the homework doesn’t stick

Kabir told you himself: being around other people is what keeps him going. That isn't distraction — it's how his attention actually works.

When he's alone, the work hasn't changed. But the thing that was quietly powering him — your presence — has gone. That's the real gap.

Kabir’s pattern

The Magnet

Attention follows connection. People light Kabir up.

The one switch

Instead of: "Go get started. I'll come check on you in a bit."

Try: "I've got something to do too. Let's both sit down and work."

Open something real — a book, a bill, anything — and settle in beside him without commenting on his work.

Try it tonight

  1. Sit down at the same time Kabir does. Bring your own task.
  2. Stay quiet. Don't check his work or ask how it's going.
  3. After a few minutes, step away briefly — then come back. Keep it normal.
