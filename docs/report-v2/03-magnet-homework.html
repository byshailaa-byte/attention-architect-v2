Kabir turns homework into a daily fight.

Here’s why, and it isn’t laziness.

In one line

Good: Kabir works hard and long when someone he trusts is nearby.

Why: Homework falls apart alone because Kabir runs on human connection, not solitude.

Fix: Be present nearby — quietly doing your own thing — while he works.

Why the homework doesn’t stick

Kabir told us people energise him. That is not a distraction — it is how he actually focuses. The same homework that stalls upstairs gets done at the kitchen table.

It is not the work that changed. It is the room. Being near someone is doing real work for Kabir.

Kabir’s pattern

The Magnet

Attention follows connection. People light Kabir up.

The one switch

Instead of: "Go upstairs and do your homework. Call me if you need help."

Try: "I've got something to do too. Let's both sit here and work."

Sit nearby with your own task. Stay present but don't check his work.

Try it tonight

  1. Pick one homework task. Sit near Kabir with something genuinely yours to do.
  2. Work quietly alongside him. Don't help, don't check — just be there.
  3. After he's settled, try stepping away briefly. Say 'I'll be right back' calmly.
